About

Composer | Audio Lead | Sound Designer

Bringing worlds to life with 20 years of audio experience - from a solo "one-man band" to leading an audio department for 9 years, including building a sound division from scratch.

I build adaptive audio systems that make sound the primary voice of the narrative - spanning dissonant soundscapes for dark atmospheric worlds, tension-building tracks, epic orchestral scores, and delicate melodies that can make a grown man cry.
