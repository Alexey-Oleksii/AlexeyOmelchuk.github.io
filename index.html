About

Audio Lead | Composer | Sound Designer

Over 20 years of experience bringing diverse worlds to life through sound — from working as a one-man audio department to leading an audio team for nine years, including building the entire audio department from the ground up.

I create adaptive audio systems where sound becomes the primary voice of the narrative — from dissonant soundscapes for dark, atmospheric worlds and music that builds psychological tension to epic orchestral scores and delicate melodies capable of moving even the toughest audiences.
